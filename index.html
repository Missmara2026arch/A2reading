👋 Story 1: Meet Carlos A1
Topic: Introductions & Personal Identification · Read the story, then do the activities.

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

💡 Tap each card to see the meaning in English and Spanish.

📖 Read the Story

My name is Carlos. I am 28 years old. I am from Mexico, but I live in Madrid now. I am married and I have one son. His name is Miguel. He is three years old.

I work as an engineer. I work in a big company near the city centre. My colleagues are very friendly. I like my job very much.

In my free time, I like to read books and cycle in the park. My favourite book is a fiction story about adventures. On weekends, I sometimes play basketball with my friends. I am happy in Madrid!

Me too! Maybe we can arrange to go cycling together sometime!
🌅 Story 2: A Typical Day A1–A2
Topic: Daily Routines & Everyday Life

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

📖 Read the Story

My name is Ana. I have a very daily routine. I wake up at seven o'clock every morning. First, I brush my teeth and take a shower. Then I have breakfast — usually toast and coffee.

I go to work by public transport. I attend a language school from nine to one. I am an English teacher. I love my students — they work hard and they are very creative.

In the afternoon, I go to the gym. I think it is important to stay fit. After the gym, I cook dinner at home. I usually cook fresh vegetables and chicken.

In the evening, I read or watch a comedy on TV. I definitely enjoy my evenings! I go to sleep at eleven o'clock.

🗣️ Conversation Practice

A
What time do you usually wake up?
B
I wake up at seven. It's my daily routine. What about you?
A
Me too! Do you go to the gym?
B
Yes, I go every afternoon. I think it's important to stay fit. Do you agree?
A
I definitely agree. I also cycle to work. It's good for your health.
B
That's a great idea! I want to be more active too.
🍕 Story 3: Lunch with Friends A2
Topic: Food, Opinions & Relationships

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

📖 Read the Story

Every Friday, Mia meets her friends for lunch. They usually go to a small restaurant near their office. Today, they have a table for four people.

Mia looks at the menu. She sees different choices: soup, pasta, and grilled beef. She wants to try something new, so she orders the beef with vegetables. Her friend Tom orders pasta because he prefers vegetarian food.

"I think the food here is excellent," says Mia. "I agree," says Tom. "The chef is very creative."

After lunch, they have coffee and a biscuit. They talk about their week. Mia says her new project is exciting but also very common in her department. Tom complains that his boss gives him too much work.

"Well," says Mia with a smile, "at least we have these lunches to look forward to!"

🗣️ Conversation Practice

A
What do you usually order for lunch?
B
I usually order pasta. I prefer vegetarian food. What about you?
A
I like beef or chicken. I think it's excellent here. Do you agree?
B
Yes, I do! The chef is very creative. I always enjoy coming here.
A
Me too. I also like the choice on the menu. There's something for everyone.
B
That's true. Shall we arrange to come again next Friday?
✈️ Story 4: A Trip to London A2
Topic: Travel & Free Time

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

📖 Read the Story

Last summer, Lucia went abroad for the first time. She travelled to London by airline from Madrid. The flight was short — only two hours.

In London, she stayed in a small hotel near the city centre. Every morning, she walked to a café for breakfast. She loved the fresh orange juice and toast.

Lucia visited many places. She went to an art gallery and saw beautiful paintings. She also walked across a famous bridge and took many photos. One afternoon, she visited a big castle — it was ancient and very attractive.

The weather was not always clear, but Lucia did not mind. She said, "I definitely want to come back. London is an amazing city!" She already has an arrangement to visit again next year.

🗣️ Conversation Practice

A
Have you ever travelled abroad?
B
Yes! I went to London last summer. I took an airline from Madrid. It was wonderful.
A
What did you do there? Did you visit any galleries or castles?
B
Yes, both! I also walked across a famous bridge. The city is very attractive.
A
I want to travel abroad too. Do you have any advice?
B
Definitely book your hotel early! And bring a camera — you'll take hundreds of photos.
🎓 Story 5: Back to School A2
Topic: Education & Hobbies

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

📖 Read the Story

David is 32 years old. Last year, he decided to go back to school. He attends a university in Barcelona. He is studying biology because he wants to become a scientist.

University life is not easy. David has to write long essays and do many experiments. He also has to attend many conferences. Sometimes he is very tired, but he continues because he loves to discover new things.

In his free time, David has some interesting hobbies. He loves drawing — he has a diary full of his drawings. He also enjoys fishing near a lake outside the city on Sundays.

David says: "I think education is the best advantage you can give yourself. I feel more able to do things now than before."

🗣️ Conversation Practice

A
Are you still studying?
B
Yes! I attend university now. I'm studying biology. It's very interesting.
A
That sounds difficult! Do you write many essays?
B
Yes, quite a lot. But I also do experiments, which I love. Do you have any hobbies?
A
I like drawing. I have a diary full of sketches. What about you?
B
I enjoy fishing on Sundays. It's very relaxing. I think hobbies are a great advantage for the mind.
💼 Story 6: My New Job A2
Topic: Work & Personal Opinions

📇 Key Vocabulary — Flip the cards!

📖 Read the Story

Rosa started a new job three months ago. She is an assistant at a design company. Her employer is a nice woman called Mrs Garcia. Rosa feels very comfortable at work.

Rosa's main department works on advertising. She helps to design advertisements for fashion brands. She needs to be very creative every day.

She earns a good amount of money, which allows her to save for a holiday abroad. She is also learning new digital skills every week.

"I definitely think this job is a big advantage for my career," says Rosa. "My colleagues are very helpful and my boss always gives good advice."

🗣️ Conversation Practice

A
How do you like your new job?
B
I love it! I'm an assistant at a design company. My employer is very kind.
A
What do you do exactly?
B
I help to design adverts for fashion brands. I need to be very creative.
A
That sounds exciting! Do you earn well?
B
Yes, a good amount. And my boss always gives me useful advice. I feel very lucky!
